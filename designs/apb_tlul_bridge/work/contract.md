## Assumptions

- Black-box only (designer decision): all checks use top-level ports. Rules about internal FSM states and registers are mapped to their effects at the ports. The checker-side flags req_open, pending and exp_get are computed only from port signals.
- Cycle naming: E0 is the rising edge that ends the Setup cycle (i_apb_psel=1, i_apb_penable=0 sampled). A1, A2, A3 are the cycles that end at E1, E2, E3. Values are sampled at rising edges, just before the edge. An Access phase of N cycles means o_apb_pready is sampled 1 at E_N. The Setup-to-Setup interval is the number of edges between consecutive Setup edges.
- The testbench APB requester follows REF-001 (ASM-001). Every transfer starts with exactly one Setup cycle; PSEL, PENABLE, PADDR, PWRITE, PWDATA and PSTRB are held until PREADY; and psel=1 with penable=0 is never sampled in the middle of a transfer. Every sampled psel=1/penable=0 is therefore treated as a Setup edge while the bridge is idle (used in CHK-005, CHK-006, CHK-007, CHK-014, CHK-011).
- Unless a test says otherwise, the TL-UL device model follows REF-005. It returns exactly one response per accepted request, no earlier than the acceptance cycle, with d_source equal to a_source and d_opcode 3'b001 for Get or 3'b000 for PutFullData. A 'prompt device' asserts i_tlul_a_ready whenever o_tlul_a_valid=1 and i_tlul_d_valid for one cycle in the cycle after acceptance.
- Reset release is synchronous (1 time unit after a rising edge), and the requester and device models are reset together with the bridge (DEP-002, BR-7.3-05). During reset the requester keeps i_apb_psel=0 from the edge before release.
- o_tlul_a_data is checked only for writes. For reads its value is unused by the device and the spec does not say whether PWDATA is loaded.
- A wrong-opcode response is consumed and reported as PSLVERR=1, PRDATA=0 (DEC-008). This is taken as the DUT's form of the host-side 'discard' in TL-T4.1.3.5-08; orphans are discarded with no APB effect.
- Tests run in the default configuration (CFG-BASE) unless rerun in another single configuration. CHK-020's wrong source uses PARA_SRC_ID XOR 1 so that it works for any PARA_SRC_W.
