1. LPARA_REQ_W has 'at least 7' minimum, but the formula ADDR_W + 3 + 1 + STRB_W + DATA_W gives a minimum of 1+3+1+1+8 = 14; is the stated minimum correct? Similarly, LPARA_RESP_W minimum 9 equals DATA_W+1 at DATA_W=8 and is consistent. (AB2-8-13, AB2-8-14) — *The document is internally inconsistent. The formula ADDR_W+3+1+STRB_W+DATA_W gives a minimum of 14 for ADDR_W=1 and DATA_W=8, but 'at least 7' is stated. The designer must say whether the stated minimum or the formula is correct. (LPARA_RESP_W minimum 9 is consistent.)*
   - **Decision** (Claude (delegated by GabrielHCMUT), 2026-11-10T19:01:03+00:00): The formula is binding: LPARA_REQ_W = ADDR_W + 3 + 1 + STRB_W + DATA_W (+ USER_REQ_W + USER_DATA_W in APB5) and LPARA_RESP_W = DATA_W + 1 (+ USER_DATA_W + USER_RESP_W in APB5), as in the parameter table and section 9. 'At least 7' is a loose lower bound that every legal configuration satisfies (the formula gives at least 14), not a separate requirement; no check is made on it. Both widths are internal and not visible at the ports.
