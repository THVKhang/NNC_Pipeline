## Assumptions

- N (synchronizer stages) = 2 for PARA_APBCDC_CELL_TYPE 1 or 2, 3 for 3, 4 for 4, and 0 for 0 (bypass, simulation only).
- The synchronizer simulation model adds 0, 1 or 2 extra destination cycles. Crossing windows are therefore [N+1, N+3] destination edges, counted from the first destination rising edge after the source edge; at coincident edges the destination samples the value from before the edge.
- The latency-bound tests (CHK-025, CHK-026) run with the model's extra latency forced to 0, because the specified bound counts only N stages.
- o_apb_prdata, o_apb_pruser and o_apb_pbuser keep their value after the PREADY cycle (only o_apb_pready and o_apb_pslverr are cleared at the RESP exit edge, per section 10.1).
- All assertions except CHK-009 and CHK-010 are disabled while i_rst_n_mst=0 or i_rst_n_slv=0.
- The requester and completer models follow APB (Issue D) except where a test explicitly breaks a rule (CHK-035 PSTRB in a read, CHK-024).
- The time at which the in-progress term of o_apb_pwakeup deasserts after a transfer is not specified; CHK-020 uses a loose bound of N+3 slv edges.
