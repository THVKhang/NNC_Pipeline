# Notice — uart_lite

**EN:** A design made from scratch by the design half of NNC Pipeline (phase 10); no upstream files. The specification `spec/uart_lite_Design_Specification.md` was drafted by agent A0 (`nnc spec-draft`) from the short description `spec/design_description.md`, then edited with the decisions D-001..D-012 and answers OQ-001..OQ-003, made on delegation (the designer is not a hardware engineer). Everything under `work/`, `verif/` and `sim/` was generated by NNC Pipeline, except two hand fixes made after triage (B: test or checker wrong, `work/triage.md` of 2026-11-18), each marked `NNC hand fix (G3, triage B)` in the file and recorded at the G3 re-approval of 2026-11-18:

- hand fix 1, `verif/tests/test_uart_lite.py` `Env.start()`: `i_rst_n` now falls before the clock starts, as ENV-002 requires; the generated harness clocked an un-reset design for three edges (CHK-002 and CHK-010 failed at 0-40 ns).
- hand fix 2, `verif/sva/nnc_chk_rx.sv` reference model: a frame starts only on a falling edge of `i_rxd` (previous sample, 1 at reset release, REQ-RST-010); the model started a frame on any low sample and invented frames on a line held low (CHK-003, CHK-005, CHK-006, CHK-009).

Neither edit removes or relaxes a check. Licence: MIT, as the repository.
