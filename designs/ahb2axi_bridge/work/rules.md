## Open questions (the designer decides: `nnc answer <design> <n> "<decision>" --by <name>`)

1. FEAT-004 is 'Required' but its aggregation is conditional and 'may' in AA-1.1-02. Is aggregation mandatory when conditions hold or optional? (AA-1.1-02, AA-1.3-04) — *The documents conflict. FEAT-004 is 'Required', and 5.3 says the bridge 'emit[s] one matching INCR/WRAP AXI burst' when attributes are constant. REQ-FUNC-005 and 1.1 say 'may', which T1 defines as optional. The designer must decide whether aggregation is mandatory whenever the conditions hold.*
   - **Decision** (Claude (delegated by GabrielHCMUT), 2026-11-12T16:11:29+00:00): Optional, per DEC-BURST-001 (§20.3, 'may aggregate'). Checks must accept both one aggregated INCR/WRAP AXI burst and single-beat AXI transfers for an eligible burst; when a burst is aggregated it must match the AHB burst (LEN 3/7/15 before split, same beats in order). Coverage records whether aggregation happened. [Delegated assumption: confirm at G4]
2. REQ-PERF-002 says 'shall meet' but also calls the 6-cycle limit a 'target'. Is it a hard checked bound or a goal? Does the count include cycles when the AHB transfer is stalled? (AA-3-41, AA-3-42) — *The text is inconsistent on whether the 6-cycle limit is a hard bound or a goal. REQ-PERF-002 says 'shall meet', but 10.4 and 13 call it a 'target', and 13 marks it 'requires implementation verification'. The designer must say whether a miss fails a check. The count starts at the accepted AHB address, so earlier AHB stall cycles are not counted. The conditions are queue space and AXI READY high.*
   - **Decision** (Claude (delegated by GabrielHCMUT), 2026-11-12T16:11:30+00:00): Hard bound under the stated conditions only, per DEC-PERF-002 (§20.3): at most 6 i_clk_core cycles from the accepted AHB address phase to AXI request issue while queue space exists and AXI READY is high; for fixed (aggregated) bursts the count starts after full capture or early termination. Cycles with READY low or no queue space are not counted; when the conditions are not met the check is not applied. [Delegated assumption: confirm at G4]
3. Does the bridge allow one read and one write outstanding simultaneously, or does the control policy serialize them? (AA-5.4-06, AA-5.3-02) — *The text says at most one read and one write are outstanding, but it does not say whether both can be outstanding at once. 10.4 says no later transaction bypasses an unresolved operation, 'when required'. Early OKAY on buffered writes may let a later read start while a write B is pending. The designer must state whether read and write are fully serialized.*
   - **Decision** (Claude (delegated by GabrielHCMUT), 2026-11-12T16:11:30+00:00): Not specified, so neither required nor forbidden: checks enforce at most one outstanding read and at most one outstanding write (AA-5.4-06) and the ordering rules of §10.4 where they apply, but no check requires or forbids a read and a write outstanding at the same time. [Delegated assumption: confirm at G4]
4. Does 'ID width remains 3' with PARA_RD_ID_COUNT up to 8 imply IDs 0..7 rotate, and what is the rotation order/reset value when count is not a power of 2? (AA-8-01, AA-8-02, AA-8-03, AA-8-04) — *The ID range is stated: 3-bit ID, rotation through 0..count-1 per direction. The documents do not give the reset or initial ID value, nor the explicit wrap order for a count that is not a power of 2. The designer must state both.*
   - **Decision** (Claude (delegated by GabrielHCMUT), 2026-11-12T16:11:30+00:00): Per DEC-ID-001 and §8: every ARID is in 0..PARA_RD_ID_COUNT-1 and every AWID in 0..PARA_WR_ID_COUNT-1 (3-bit fields); with count 1 the ID is always 0. The reset value and the exact rotation order are not specified, so no check assumes them; coverage records that every legal ID value is used. [Delegated assumption: confirm at G4]
5. After boundary split, what are the AWLEN values of the split bursts? Only pre-split lengths are given. (AA-9-11, AA-9-09) — *The documents give only pre-split lengths (LEN 3, 7 or 15). They do not say how a split burst divides into sub-burst lengths. The designer must define the AWLEN of each sub-burst.*

[Delegated assumption: confirm at G4]

(AA-14.3-03, AA-14.3-02) — *The FIFO contract (interface, depth, reset behavior) is delegated to the external 002_SyncFIFO spec (REF-004), which is not in these documents. ## Questions answered by the documents

- Does 'Reads always use AXI single-beat' conflict with aggregating or burst handling of AHB read bursts? Clarify how AHB read bursts map to multiple single-beat AXI reads. → No conflict. Every AHB read, including INCR4/8/16 and WRAP4/8/16, issues one single-beat AXI read (ARLEN=0) for each accepted beat. Reads are never aggregated. [1.1:s19, T1.7.3.1:s10, 3:s8, 15.2:s2]
- Early AHB OKAY on buffered Normal Write-back burst beats: is early OKAY permitted or required for non-final beats, and is a later BRESP error reported only on the terminal beat? → Early OKAY for non-final, safely buffered beats is permitted ('may'), not required. A later B error is reported only on the terminal AHB beat, and the terminal beat waits for the required B response(s). Earlier buffered beats stay acknowledged. [3:s11, 5.3:s8, 12:s5, T1.7.3.4:s3]
- When is a burst aggregated vs single-beat, and when can defensive splitting for 4 KiB occur given fixed-length 4/8/16 bursts? → A burst is aggregated only when it is a complete, legal bufferable Normal Write-back fixed burst (4/8/16) whose AXI address attributes are constant, and only after all beats are captured. Otherwise it uses ordered singles. Device-E and non-bufferable writes are always singles. The 4 KiB split is only defensive, for an out-of-profile address sequence that crosses 4 KiB. A compliant AHB burst does not need it. [3:s9, 3:s14, 5.3:s5, 5.3:s6, T1.7.3.1:s12, T1.7.3.1:s18, T1.7.3.1:s19]
- What reset behavior is required for in-flight transactions (no pre-reset response after release)? It is not extracted as a rule. → Reset clears all bridge and FIFO valid state. In-flight commands, data and responses are discarded and the AXI VALIDs drop. No pre-reset response may be returned after release, provided the connected AXI endpoint or interconnect is reset consistently. [3:s17, 7.3:s1, 7.3:s6, 10.2:s6]
- What counts as an 'unexpected' EXOKAY, and what are the 'required BRESPs' for a fixed write burst (one per AW or per beat)? → EXOKAY is 'unexpected' because no exclusive accesses are issued, so every EXOKAY is treated as an error and maps to AHB ERROR. The required BRESPs are one per AXI write transaction issued. That is one for an aggregated burst, one per sub-burst if split, and one per beat for ordered singles. [3:s18, T1.7.3.4:s6, 5.3:s3, 5.3:s8, 5.3:s13, 3:s14, 3:s13]
- What are the legal ranges for each parameter of m_ahb2axi_top? The quote says out-of-range values are elaboration errors but does not list the ranges. Should the elaboration error be a tool-level failure, such as a generate-time $error, or is any rejection acceptable? → PARA_RD_ID_COUNT and PARA_WR_ID_COUNT are the only parameters. Both have default 1 and legal range 1..8. Out-of-range values are elaboration errors. The text does not say how the error is raised (generate-time $error or otherwise). [8:s1, 8:s2, 4:s5, 8:s4]
- What happens if a buffered beat that already completed OKAY later receives an error B response? → Earlier buffered beats stay acknowledged with OKAY. The B error is reported on the terminal AHB beat, which reflects the status of all B responses. [3:s11, 12:s5, T1.7.3.4:s3]
- IF-003 says reset is asynchronous-assert with SoC-synchronized release before the IP boundary. Does the bridge need to tolerate/verify asynchronous assertion mid-transfer (AHB/AXI outputs state during reset), or is release synchronization purely an integration assumption outside verification scope? → Release synchronization is a SoC integration assumption, and the bridge has no reset synchronizer. Asynchronous assertion is part of the contract and must be verified. During reset, HREADYOUT=1, HRESP=OKAY, AW/W/ARVALID=0 and BREADY/RREADY=0, and in-flight state is dropped. [1.2:s4, 3:s17, 7.3:s1, 7.3:s3, 10.2:s6, 15.4:s3]
- IF-001 and IF-002 direction fields list both directions; is the intended clock domain single (i_clk_core) for both interfaces with no CDC, and is the table's 'Signal type' sentence (Vietnamese text) part of the normative requirement? → Yes. Both interfaces use the single i_clk_core domain, with no CDC. The English text is normative and the Vietnamese text restates the same intent for review, so it is not an independent requirement. [6.1:s1, 6.1:s2, 6.2:s2, 7.2:s1, T1:s2, T1:s3]
- AA-T1.7.2.2-12/14/22 hold-until-READY and AA-T1.7.3.2-01: does 'hold' include payload stability only while VALID high, and is reset exempt? → VALID and payload must stay stable until the READY handshake. The payload is only qualified by its VALID. Reset is the exception: it clears VALID and discards in-flight state, and the combinational adapter suppresses VALID during reset. [T1.7.3.2:s1, 12:s6, 5.1:s5, 7.3:s1, 10.2:s6, 15.3:s1]
- HMASTLOCK=1 is rejected with ERROR but i_ahb_hmastlock reset/idle value is 0; confirm whether ERROR applies to all transfers with HMASTLOCK=1 regardless of other fields. → Yes. Any accepted transfer with HMASTLOCK=1 is rejected with a two-cycle AHB ERROR and no AXI request. The reset/idle value of 0 only applies to the idle input state. [3:s19, T1.7.3.1:s28, 12:s1, 20.3:s6]
- The 4 KiB defensive split vs 1 KiB AHB rule: is a 1 KiB-crossing burst by a manager an illegal stimulus or a tested defensive scenario? → A 1 KiB-crossing burst is out-of-profile stimulus: the manager must not start it. The 4 KiB split is a defensive behavior that does not make such a burst compliant. It is still a tested scenario (TEST-08, WF-006). [T1.7.3.1:s17, T1.7.3.1:s19, 15.2:s8, 10.3:s12]
- Does the AXI ID count apply per direction and how is it configured? Rotation after each completed transaction conflicts with 'count > 1 does not enable concurrency'. Is the ID checked at each address handshake or at response? → ID counts are per direction and set by the static parameters PARA_RD_ID_COUNT and PARA_WR_ID_COUNT, range 1..8. Each direction rotates through 0..count-1 after each completed AXI transaction. There is no conflict, because a count above 1 does not enable concurrent transactions. The ID is checked against the configured count (15.3). The text does not specify at which handshake it is checked, and it leaves a mismatched response ID undefined. Which behavior applies, and what is the intended response for an incomplete burst? → The two statements do not conflict. Aggregation is optional ('may'), but its precondition is that the full burst is captured and the attributes are uniform. An incomplete or early-terminated burst is never sent as an AXI burst. Its captured, unissued beats go out as ordered singles, and the terminal AHB response carries the aggregated B status. [3:s9, 3:s13, T1.7.3.1:s14, T1.7.3.3:s22, T1.7.3.4:s3]

Device, non-cacheable, write-through and non-bufferable writes stay single-beat. [T1.7.3.2:s6, T1.7.3.3:s18, T1.7.3.3:s19, T1.7.3.3:s20, T1.7.3.3:s21, T1.7.3.3:s22, T1.7.3.1:s12, T1.7.3.4:s2]
