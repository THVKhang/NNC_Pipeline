## Assumptions

- Sampling convention: assertion values at 'edge n' are the values in the cycle ending at edge n (sampled just before the rising edge of i_clk); a value required 'from edge n' is checked at edge n+1 and later.
- Event names follow the spec terms: Ea/Ew = edges of the AW/W transfers, Ec = the later of Ea and Ew, Eb = B transfer edge, Ear = AR transfer edge, Er = R transfer edge, R1 = first rising edge with i_rst_n=1 after the most recent reset.
- Decode used by the checkers: word index w = addr[PARA_ADDR_W-1:2]; w < PARA_NUM_REGS selects REG_w, w == 16 selects STATUS, every other w is unmapped.
- All checkers are disabled before the first assertion of i_rst_n and reset all their tracking state whenever i_rst_n=0.
- The simulator is 2-state (Verilator), so X/Z on inputs is prevented by the testbench and not checked.
- CHK-017 is done by monitoring value changes on the outputs and comparing their simulation time with i_clk rising-edge times and i_rst_n falling-edge times.
