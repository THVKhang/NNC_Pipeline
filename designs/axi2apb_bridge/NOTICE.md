**EN:** The files are unchanged copies, except `rtl/m_axi2apb_wr_seq.sv`, modified on 2026-11-08 to fix the write-address bug described in [fix_candidate/README.md](fix_candidate/README.md) (the change is marked in the file header). The specification cites the Arm AMBA AXI (IHI 0022H.c) and APB (IHI 0024D) clauses without reproducing them; those standards are licensed by Arm and are **not** registered as documents of this design, so the rules come from the IP specification only. A user who holds the standards can add them locally (`nnc doc add ... --role standard`); results that quote them must then stay out of git. Everything under `work/` and `verif/` was generated by NNC Pipeline. One hand fix of generated verification code, recorded at the G3 re-approval of 2026-11-12: formal counterexamples showed that the read checker `verif/sva/nnc_chk_axi_r.sv` classified three illegal requests as supported (FIXED bursts above 16 beats, AB-3-07; the 4-KiB range from an unaligned start, AXI A3.4; an unaligned WRAP start, AXI A3.4), while the RTL rejected them as the specification requires; only that classification was changed.
