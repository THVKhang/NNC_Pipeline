## Assumptions

- AXI4 encodings are used: RESP OKAY=2'b00 and SLVERR=2'b10; BURST FIXED=2'b00, INCR=2'b01, WRAP=2'b10, and 2'b11 is reserved.
- A supported request with no PSLVERR returns OKAY: BRESP=2'b00, and RRESP=2'b00 with RDATA equal to PRDATA. The spec states this only implicitly, through 'never report failure as OKAY' and the normal conversion.
- Request types whose response class the spec leaves unclear are never generated, and the checkers make no claim about their response class: FIXED with AxLEN>15, WRAP with AxLEN not in {1,3,7,15}, WRAP with a start address not aligned to 2^AxSIZE, and malformed WLAST or WSTRB.
- 4-KiB crossing applies only to INCR bursts: AxADDR[31:12] != ((AxADDR & ~(2^AxSIZE-1)) + (AxLEN+1)*2^AxSIZE - 1)[31:12]. FIXED and legal WRAP bursts cannot cross.
- Some timing is not stated in the spec, so the checks accept these same-edge cases: o_axi_wready may be 1 at the same edge as the AW handshake; a new AR/AW handshake may coincide with the final R or B handshake of the previous transaction; o_apb_psel may be 1 at the address-handshake edge; an R beat may be presented at the same edge as its APB completion. BVALID must still come strictly after the last W handshake and the last APB completion.
- 'APB idle' (outputs 0) means o_apb_psel=0.
- The ready outputs (o_axi_awready/wready/arready) are required to be 0 only when i_rst_n_axi is sampled 0 at two consecutive edges; ready may rise combinationally in the first cycle after reset is released.
- A transaction counts as 'completed' for the round-robin preference flip at its final R handshake (o_axi_rlast=1) or its B handshake, and this includes unsupported (SLVERR) transactions.
- Tests run with PARA_AXI_ID_W=4 (CFG-BASE).
